# Governance Model

## 1. Introduction

Commputing is designed to support the organization and administration of many different leftist political systems.

It's primary governance model is inspired by Marxism and Marxism-Leninism,
particularly the ideas of democratic centralism,
collective ownership, worker's councils, and planned economic coordination.

That being said Commputing is not designed to enforce one political system.
Different movements, organizations, and governments have different ideas
about how society should be organized. The software should be flexible enough to accommodate these differences.

The goal is to provide the tools necessary to organize, manage, and coordinate
these systems with minimal restriction on how they operate.

## 2. Political Foundations

The default governance model will draw inspiration from several Marxist and socialist principles.

### Marxism

The projects general political foundation is based on Marxist ideas, including: 

- Collective ownership of the means of production
- The importance of class relations
- The organization of production around social needs
- The reduction of economic exploitation and inequality
- The development of society beyond capitalism

### Marxism-Leninism

The project will additionally draw from Marxist-Leninist principals, including:

- Democratic centralism
- Organized political leadership
- worker's and people's councils
- coordinated economic planning
- collective political decision-making

These principals will influence how software is developed but
will not be mandatory for every implementation

## 3. Government Structure

Commputing should support multiple levels of administration.

The base configuration will use three administrative levels.

### Local Administration

Local institutions will handle matters directly affecting their communities.

Responsibilities could include:

- Local public services
- Community infrastructure
- Housing administration
- Local resource management
- Community assemblies
- Local economic coordination

Local institutions should have authority over matters within their juristiction.

### Regional Administration

Regional institutions will coordinate activities between local governments and communities.

Responsibilities could include: 

- Regional infrastructure
- Transportation networks
- Resource coordination
- Regional economic planning
- Coordination between local institutions
- Regional public services

### National Administration

National institutions will coordinate matters affecting the country as a whole.

Responsibilities may include:

- National economic planning
- National infrastructure
- International relations
- National legislation
- Coordination of major public services
- National resource distribution

## 4. Democratic Organization

The base model will support a system of representative councils and assemblies.

These institutions should allow workers and citizens to participate in political and administrative decision-making.

The software should support:

- Electing representatives
- Holding assemblies and meetings
- Submittings proposals
- Recording votes and decisions
- Publishing meeting records
- Tracking the implementation of decisions
- Recalling representative3s where the governing system provides for it

Different implementations should be able to determine how representatives are selected,
how long they serve, and what responsibilities they hold.

## 5. Democratic centralism

Democratic centralism is an important influence on the default Marxist-Leninist configuration.

Commputing should provide tools that support both collective
discussion and coordinated implementation of decisions.

This may include:

- Discussion and proposal submission before decisions
- Formal voting and decision recording
- Clear documentation of adopted decisions
- Coordination between administrative levels
- Monitoring the implementation of policies
- Procedures for reviewing and revising decisions

The software should distinguish between the process of reaching
a decision and the process of implementing it.

It should also support configurable safeguards, including
transparent voting procedures, defined authority, independent
review, and mechanisms for challenging decisions.

## 6. Political Organizations

Commputing should support the participation of political parties,
workers' organizations, trade unions, cooperatives, and other political or social organizations.

Depending on the chosen governance model, these organizations
may have different responsibilities.

The software should support:

- Organizational membership management
- Internal elections
- Meetings and conferences
- Policy proposals
- Internal voting
- Organizational records
- Communication between affiliated organizations

Political organizations should not automatically receive
administrative powers just because they exist within the platform.
Their authority should be defined by the chosen governance model.

## 7. Economic Governance

Economic planning is one of the central purposes of Commputing.

The platform should eventually provide tools for managing
economic activity and coordinating resources across different
levels of society.

This includes:

- Production planning
- Resource allocation
- Supply and demand tracking
- Inventory management
- Distribution coordination
- Public enterprise administration
- Economic statistics and reporting
- Monitoring shortages and surpluses

Different economic models should be supported, including centralized planning,
decentralized planning, cooperative ownership, and combinations of these approaches.

## 8. Public Participation and Accountability

A functioning governance system requires ways for people to
participate in decisions and hold institutions accountable.

Commputing should provide tools that allow citizens and workers to:

- Submit proposals and petitions
- Participate in public consultations
- Vote where applicable
- Access public records
- Review government budgets
- Monitor public projects
- Report administrative problems
- Submit complaints and appeals
- Review the performance of public institutions

Participation mechanisms should be accessible and designed to
prevent manipulation, coercion, and unauthorized interference.

## 9. Adaptability to Different Leftist Ideologies

Although the default configuration is Marxist-Leninist,
Commputing should support a wide range of leftist political traditions.

Potential configurations include:

- Marxism-Leninism
- Democratic socialism
- Libertarian socialism
- Council communism
- Anarcho-communism
- Market socialism
- Syndicalism
- Other socialist and communist traditions

These configurations may differ in their approaches to political authority,
economic planning, ownership, representation, and decision-making.

The software should allow these differences to be represented through configurable institutions,
permissions, procedures, and economic systems.

No single governance configuration should be permanently embedded into the underlying software.

## 10. Configurable Governance Architecture

Governance structures should be implemented as configurable components.

Administrators should be able to define:

- Administrative levels
- Government departments
- Political institutions
- Representative structures
- Election procedures
- Voting systems
- Decision-making processes
- Authority and permissions
- Economic planning structures
- Public participation mechanisms

Changes to governance configurations should be documented and auditable.

The system should also maintain historical records so that changes to institutions,
responsibilities, and procedures can be understood over time.

## 11. Transparency and Safeguards

Government administration requires accountability and protection against abuses of authority.

Commputing should inlcude safeguards such as:

- Auditable administrative actions
- Clearly defined institutional authority
- Public access to non-sensitive government records
- Independent oversight mechanisms
- Documented decision-making procedures
- Protection of personal information
- Secure and verifiable elections
- Procedures for appeals and complaints

Not all government information should necessarily be public.
Personal records, sensitive information,
and legitimate security-related material should receive appropriate protection.

## 12. Long-Term Objective

The long-term objective is to make Commputing capable of
supporting a wide range of socialist and communist governance systems
through one adaptable software platform.

Marxism-Leninism will be the primary reference model during
intitial development, while underlying architecture will remain flexible
enough to accomodate other approches.

Ultimatly, Commputing should provide infrastructure needed to organize
institutions, coordinate economic activity, manage public services,
and facilitate collective decision-making.
